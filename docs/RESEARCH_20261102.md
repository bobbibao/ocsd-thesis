The prior 12-scene screen supports a sparse-M2 efficiency improvement, not state-of-the-art quality. The subsequent semantic router failed the development comparison. ## Related work checked against official repositories

Sources retrieved on 2026-11-02; this is a targeted review, **not an exhaustive 2026 state-of-the-art survey**:

- [InstanceDiffusion (CVPR 2024)](https://github.com/frank-xwang/InstanceDiffusion), repository HEAD `722900ef03e3579fb693e65a1550333a4a162581`: paired instance language with points, scribbles, boxes and masks, and iterative editing. Its README describes a reimplementation with potential differences from the paper. Its diffusion-library example uses a custom fork; the installed pinned diffusers 0.40.0 does not expose `StableDiffusionINSTDIFFPipeline`. Integration requires an isolated, pinned environment and parity checks, not replacing this environment with an untested fork.
- [MIGC / MIGC++](https://github.com/limuloo/MIGC), repository HEAD `effd6cc2e6a9f7d2fe34409c300a5f037a87b9ec`: multi-instance layout/attribute control, iterative editing, and released mask+box control. Relevant to both binding and placement. Its README links COCO-MIG and the CVPR 2024/TPAMI papers. No performance from those papers is presented as our reproduced result.
- GLIGEN remains a useful box-conditioned control, but outperforming GLIGEN alone would not establish best performance for sketch-plus-text. The arXiv page request was blocked by the environment; no claim of a fresh full-paper review is made.

An object-centric repair design is therefore not automatically novel. Any eventual contribution must demonstrate an advantage over instance-level methods, not merely rename known local-conditioning concepts. Neither InstanceDiffusion nor MIGC was run here. Their reported scores are not directly comparable to our synthetic 12-scene means.

## What a defensible paper still requires

1. Freeze the input contract: raw sketch+caption versus supplied instance annotations. Evaluate parsing errors separately; do not conceal oracle boxes/masks as inferred input.
2. Test simultaneously correct instances/count/layout/relations, plus shape fidelity and image quality. Add original sketches to human review; box mIoU alone cannot measure shape.
3. Use disjoint development and final test scenes, multiple seeds, real/user sketches, uncertainty estimates and independent blinded judgments. Do not reuse the inspected 12 scenes as final evidence.
4. Compare with strong instance/scribble-conditioned baselines under disclosed backbone, resolution, sampling and runtime budgets. Measure both total actual compute and reconstructed standalone costs.
5. Preserve failed experiments and gate further spending on meaningful gains. No engineering change guarantees a paper score, publication acceptance, or a globally best model.
